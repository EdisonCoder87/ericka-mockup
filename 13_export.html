Xero sales invoice (CSV)
Import at Business → Invoices → Import. Every line carries the same invoice number, so Xero builds one invoice to SIA Medical with a line per person. Check the contact name matches Xero exactly or it will create a new one.
💸
Contractor payout (CSV)
Save as hours_<paydate>.csv in ericka-bpo/payroll/ and run build_wise_batch.py. Covers everyone for the period, not just the client selected above — we pay the whole team in one run. Hours only; the pay rate lives in va_roster.csv, never in the portal.

Shift-by-shift detail (CSV)
Every clock record behind the totals, with who edited what. Keep it with the invoice — this is what answers a query about a line six weeks later.
